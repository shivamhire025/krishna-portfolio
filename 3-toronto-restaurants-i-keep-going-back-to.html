3 Toronto Restaurants I Keep Going Back To

The reliable spots: where the food is consistently amazing, the atmosphere just fits, and you never leave disappointed.

KT
Written by Krishna Thakkar·August 2026·4 min read

If there is one thing about Toronto's food scene, it's that it can be overwhelming in the best way possible. Every week there is a new spot trending, a new patio opening up, or a menu going viral. But when Friday night actually rolls around, most of us end up leaning on the reliable spots, the places where the food is consistently amazing, the atmosphere just fits, and you never leave disappointed.

Whether I'm craving something fresh after a long week, hunting down serious spice, or looking for a dim-lit patio for drinks with friends, these are three Toronto spots I genuinely keep coming back to.

1

Fresh Kitchen + Juice Bar

Danforth, near The Big Carrot

A natural, sunlit shot of a colorful grain bowl and fresh juice sitting on a simple wooden tabletop.

I'll be honest: I don't always eat green, but when I'm feeling like I need a full reset, Fresh is the absolute first place on my list. Tucked along the Danforth right near The Big Carrot, it's one of those spots that proves plant-based food doesn't have to feel boring or restrictive.

Their menu is massive, but the real star here is how they handle comfort food. You can get a massive bowl loaded with crispy tofu, brown rice, avocado, and house-made dressings that actually pack a punch. Paired with one of their cold-pressed juices, it's the kind of meal that makes you feel good about what you just ate without leaving you hungry an hour later.

Go-to order: A custom grain bowl topped with crispy tofu and extra sauce, plus a cold-pressed green juice.
  • Hummus platter
  • Buffalo cauliflower
  • Their famous hot sauce
Vibe: Bright, relaxed, and perfect for a casual lunch or healthy weekend catch-up.
2

Angara Indian & Hakka Restaurant

Queen Street

A cozy, warm shot of piping hot curry in a serving dish next to garlic-brushed naan and noodles.

If you love food that leans heavily into intense spices, rich gravies, and serious heat, Angara on Queen Street is an absolute gem. It's one of those places you go when you want real comfort food that hits you the second you walk through the door.

What makes Angara special is that they bridge traditional Indian curries with Indo-Chinese Hakka dishes. The flavors are deep, bold, and unapologetic. The paneer is soft, the gravies are rich and extra saucy, and their Hakka noodles have that perfect smoky, high-heat wok flavor that's almost impossible to recreate at home.

Go-to order: Paneer angara and paneer lababdar, garlic naan, and a plate of Hakka noodles for the table.
Vibe: Unpretentious, warm, and built for sharing a giant feast with friends.
3

Bar Poet

Queen Street West

A dim, cozy interior framed by brick walls, canopy lights, and indoor trees.

Bar Poet is hands-down one of my favorite places in the city when I just want a cool, low-key night out. The second you step inside, the atmosphere does all the work. It's dimly lit, framed by brick walls, and covered in twinkling canopy lights and indoor trees. It feels like eating in an outdoor courtyard, even in the middle of winter.

The food keeps things simple: incredible wood-fired sourdough pizza, great drinks, and small plates. It's not trying to be overly fancy, it's just delicious pizza in a space where you actually want to sit and talk for hours.

Go-to order: A freshly baked wood-fired pizza with extra house dip, paired with a cold draft beer.
Vibe: Dim, cozy, and effortlessly cool. Perfect for date night or hanging out late into the evening.
