Passions: Content & Food

Hi! I'm Krishna, a freelancer helping small brands come to life online. I create content, design visuals, and build strategies that make brands connect with their audience in meaningful ways.

I love working with passionate small businesses, whether it's a cosy restaurant, a budding lifestyle brand, or any creative venture. Food has a special place in my heart, and I also work part-time as a chef, which fuels my creativity and hands-on approach.

One day, I hope to open my own restaurant, blending my love for storytelling, strategy, and culinary art. 🍽️
