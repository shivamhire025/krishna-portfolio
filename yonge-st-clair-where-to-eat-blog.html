Where to Eat and Drink Right Now Near Yonge and St. Clair

Ask five people what to eat around here and you'll get five different answers, which is usually the sign of a neighbourhood doing food right.

KT
Written by Krishna Thakkar·August 2026·4 min read

A street-level view of the kind of spot that makes this stretch of Yonge worth a slow dinner.

Ask five people what to eat around Yonge and St. Clair and you'll get five different answers, which is usually the sign of a neighbourhood doing food right. Here's where locals actually go, no gimmicks, just the spots that keep tables full on a Tuesday.

For a proper sit-down Italian dinner, Capocaccia Trattoria on Yonge Street has been a fixture since 2004, known for thin-crust, wood-burning pizza and a covered patio tucked along Balmoral Avenue that feels like a break from the intersection just steps away. It's the kind of place that works for a first date or a Tuesday night, which is harder to pull off than it sounds.

A few minutes south on St. Clair, Union Social Eatery leans casual but polished: a well-crafted menu, a strong cocktail list, and the kind of room that suits both a quick bite and a long dinner with friends.

"It's not a highlight reel. It's just a genuinely solid neighbourhood food scene."

Nearby, Mary Be Kitchen takes a different approach entirely with build-your-own "DIY plates," built around whole, seasonal ingredients, and a menu that treats vegetarian options as the main event rather than an afterthought. If you're after something with more spice, Ambiyan on Yonge offers a modern take on Indian cuisine: tandoor-cooked dishes, slow-cooked curries, and a wine and cocktail list built to match.
